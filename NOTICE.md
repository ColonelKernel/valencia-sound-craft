# What the MIT license covers, and what it does not

[`LICENSE`](LICENSE) is kept as the unmodified MIT text so GitHub can detect it.
This file is the scope note that goes with it.

## Covered by MIT

The **source code** of this repository: the TypeScript, React components, build
plugins, test suites and configuration. Reuse it freely.

## Not covered — © 2026 Zach Scheffler, all rights reserved

The **written and creative content**:

- Prose and written content — the case studies in `src/pages/*CaseStudy.tsx`,
  the CV text and biography in `src/content/cv.ts`, and the copy throughout
  `src/content/` and the page components.
- The curriculum vitae and its generated PDF (`Zach-Scheffler-CV.pdf`).
- Images and brand assets, including `public/og-image.png`.
- Music, audio recordings, and the recording credits in `src/content/work.ts`.

A personal site is largely a piece of writing about one person. Licensing that
permissively would be a different act from sharing the components that render
it. Please don't republish the writing, the CV or the music as your own.
